Services

What the firm handles, and how.

Every engagement below is handled by Jon Burbage directly. There is no associate layer between the person you hire and the person who writes your claims.

01 · Preparation

Patent preparation for artificial intelligence and software inventions

The firm drafts utility applications for machine learning models and training pipelines, software systems and architectures, distributed and vehicular systems, wireless and connected-device technology, and the hardware those systems run on.

Drafting starts with an invention disclosure session with the engineers, not with a form. The object of that session is to find the technical mechanism that produces the claimed advantage, because under current Federal Circuit law that mechanism is the only thing that makes the claim eligible. Where the advantage is a resource or efficiency gain, it goes into the claim language itself, which is what Ex parte Desjardins requires.

02 · Prosecution

Prosecution and eligibility strategy

Office action responses, examiner interviews, continuation and divisional strategy, and appeals when the record is better than the examiner’s position. Eligibility rejections get the Director’s August 2025 memorandum cited on the record and the examiner held to the evidentiary burden it imposes.

The firm has kept a zero missed deadline record since 2016, on a docketing system built for that purpose.

03 · Appeals

Patent Trial and Appeal Board

Appeal briefs, reply briefs and oral argument. An appeal is a decision about the whole family, not one application, so it is taken with the continuation strategy and the client budget on the table at the same time.

04 · Portfolio and transactions

Portfolio management, searching, and diligence

Portfolio management

Filing programs matched to a product roadmap and an annual budget, foreign filing and national phase decisions, annuity and pruning decisions.

Patentability searching

Search and written analysis before money is committed to a full application.

Clearance and freedom to operate

Analysis of whether a planned product can ship, and what design changes narrow the exposure.

Intellectual property due diligence

Portfolio review for acquisitions and venture financings, on the schedule the transaction actually runs on.

05 · Counsel

Outside general counsel for technology companies

For software and artificial intelligence companies without in-house counsel, the firm acts as outside general counsel: intellectual property strategy, commercial and vendor contracts, trade secret protection, employment and confidentiality matters, and the questions that reach a board.

This work is priced to be usable by a company that is still raising money. Ask about fixed fees for defined pieces of work.
