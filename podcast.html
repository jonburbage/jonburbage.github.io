October 21: The lease clause that built the Globe

In December 1598 the Burbage brothers took apart the Theatre, London's early playhouse, and used its timber to build the Globe. Their landlord, Giles Allen, sued for years over a lease that let them remove the building only before its term ended. Jon and Catie on the clause, the deadline, and what it means for every contract right that expires on a date.

2026-10-19 · Monday · Developments · 7 min

October 19: The Board's appeal odds, invention records, and two AI filings

The Patent Trial and Appeal Board's fiscal year 2026 appeal numbers and what they mean for artificial intelligence applications; Google v. Valtrus Innovations (Fed. Cir. Oct. 19, 2026) and why invention records should stay in their native files; OpenAI's application on the prompt-box control that sets a token budget; and Google's application on sizing a model to a compute budget.

Written entries in this episode

  • The Board closed fiscal year 2026 with its smallest appeal inventory in fifteen years and a 38 percent reversal rate in the software technology center
  • The invention record that survives a priority fight is the native file with its metadata, not the copy the inventor exported
  • OpenAI claims the control in the prompt box that sets how many tokens the model may spend on an answer, and the Office allowed it before it published
  • Google claims the procedure for sizing a model's depth and width to a compute budget, filed five days before its authors posted the paper
