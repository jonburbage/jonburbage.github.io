Patents

Issued United States patents

342 United States patents have issued from applications Jon Burbage wrote, from May 2012 through June 2026. They are listed below by technology. Each title links to the patent’s full text on Google Patents. Past results do not predict the outcome of any other matter.

Two portfolios

Long-running patent programs

Toyota

240 patents for Toyota companies in Japan and the United States, from applications filed beginning in 2011. The subject is connected and automated vehicles: cars that pool their computing into shared “micro clouds,” vehicle-to-vehicle radio and radar, digital twins and simulation, driver assistance, and augmented-reality heads-up displays. The applications came from more than fifty different lead inventors.

Jaunt

48 patents for Jaunt, a virtual reality camera and content company, from applications filed beginning in 2014: the camera rig and its modules, including nine design patents; image stitching; compression and streaming of three-dimensional video; and virtual reality content and social features. Twenty-one of the 48 issued in 2020 and 2021, after the portfolio passed to Verizon.
